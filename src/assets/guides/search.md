The search box at the top searches the Content page. It understands the same search language as gwasi, so searches you already know work here too. Plain words still work: type a few words and you get posts that have all of them.

Prefer a form? Click **Expanded search** above the posts and fill in the boxes; it writes the search for you.

## Words, phrases and tags

| Type this | To find |
|---|---|
| `script offer` | posts with both words, anywhere in the title, text or credits |
| `"slow burn"` | that exact phrase |
| `[F4M]` | posts with that tag in the title |
| `/\bass\b/` | a regular expression (here: "ass" as a whole word, not "assumption") |

## Combining

| Type this | To find |
|---|---|
| `fdom OR mdom` | either word |
| `-blood` or `NOT blood` | leave out posts with that word |
| `-[blood]` | leave out posts with that tag |
| `(fdom OR mdom) aftercare` | brackets group words together |

## Who and where

| Type this | To find |
|---|---|
| `u:name` | posts by that creator |
| `w:name` | posts crediting that writer (fills of their scripts) |
| `title:word` | the word in the title only |
| `f:offer` | posts whose flair contains "offer" |
| `type:audio`, `type:script`, `type:other` | audios, Script Offers, or everything else (short: `t:a`, `t:s`, `t:o`) |
| `c:name` | posts in that cummunity |

Names with spaces go in quotes: `w:"Some Writer"`.

## Numbers

Numbers can be written `5`, `<5`, `>5`, `<=5`, `>=5`, `5+` (5 or more) or `3-10` (from 3 to 10).

| Type this | To find |
|---|---|
| `fills:0` | Script Offers nobody has filled yet |
| `fills:5+` | Script Offers with 5 fills or more |
| `userfills:>20` | posts by creators whose scripts have more than 20 fills in total |
| `length:10-20` | audios of 10 to 20 minutes, or scripts of 10,000 to 20,000 words |
| `reactions:10+` | posts with at least 10 reactions (`score:` works too) |
| `comments:>5` | posts with more than 5 comments |
| `karma:100+` | posts by creators with at least 100 reactions on all their posts |

A fill counts for a Script Offer when the filler picks the script on the post form, or when the fill links to the Script Offer's post (or its script link).

Lengths come from a length tag in the title, like [16:10] or [28 min], or from the creator's masterlist; scripts are counted when the script is posted on the site. Posts without a known length don't show up in `length:` searches.

## Dates

| Type this | To find |
|---|---|
| `date:14d` | the last 14 days (`h` hours, `d` days, `m` months, `y` years) |
| `date:2024` | posted in 2024 |
| `date:2024-11` | posted in November 2024 |
| `date:2024-2-10_2024-2-15` | posted from 10 to 15 February 2024 |

## Popular tags

The buttons above the posts are the most used tags, with how many posts have them. Click one to add it to your search, and again to take it out.

## Your saved search

Logged in, you can save a search that is always added on the Content page, for example `-[blood] -u:someone` to never see that tag or creator. Open **Expanded search**, write it under **Saved search**, and untick **Use it** whenever you want to see everything.

## Compact view

**Compact** shows one line per post (date, creator, flair, length, comments and title), handy for long lists. **Cards** goes back to the usual view. The site remembers your choice in this browser.
