audioprn isn't in the app stores, because they don't allow adult sites. Instead you can add it to your phone's home screen. It then opens full screen like any other app, with no browser bar, and always shows the latest version of the site.

## Android

1. Open audioprn.net in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
3. Tap **Install app** (on some phones: **Add to Home screen**), then **Install**.

The audioprn icon is now on your home screen and in your app list.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open audioprn.net in **Safari** (other browsers on iPhone may not offer this).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

## Computer

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then **Install**.

## Good to know

- You stay logged in, and your Settings are the same as on the website.
- To remove it, delete the app like any other: press and hold its icon.
- Other Lemmy apps (Voyager, Jerboa, Thunder…) don't show audioprn's reactions, credits, awards or masterlists. Use this one instead.
